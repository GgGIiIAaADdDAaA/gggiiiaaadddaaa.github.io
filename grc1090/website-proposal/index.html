Final project proposal

Introduction

Siren Shell Shop

This will be a shell shop that will sell local and sometimes exotic shells or some from other states depending on my travel schedule. This will be based in California along the central coast and will also sell art supplies. All the shells sold here will soley be sourced by me to make sure that they are collected ethically and lawfully.

Target audience

This store will be for crafters who need shells or collectors who canno travel themselves and do not want to order them from overseas and risk damage. It can also be for tourists who want to take a momento home with them to remind them of their trip to California.

The people who use this site have the goal of getting quality shells that are real for good prices and to make sure the shells are collected correctly. They will most likely be people who care about the ocean and marine life.
